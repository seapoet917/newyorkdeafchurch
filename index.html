Why This Matters

Deaf individuals in Mexico are among the most spiritually and socially isolated communities in Latin America. Many have never heard the Gospel — not because they reject it, but because it has never been presented in sign language.

The Mexico Deaf Family Faith Retreat is the answer. It brings together deaf families from the Cancún region for 4 days of sign language worship, Bible teaching, and community — the first gathering of its kind in eastern Mexico.

멕시코 농인들에게 예수님을 만나는 방법, 예배의 방법을 전하는 신앙의 씨앗을 뿌리는 역사적 시작입니다.

Where Your Donation Goes

100% of donations go directly to serving deaf families in Mexico. Pastors, teachers, children's teachers, and volunteer staff serve without compensation — your donation covers the essential costs that make this gathering possible.

🏠 Venue — 100 Participants

Facility rental for a gathering of up to 100 deaf family members: main hall, classrooms, dining hall, kitchen, and outdoor spaces for the full 4-day retreat.

🍽️ All Meals Included

Three daily meals for all participants throughout the 4 days are fully funded by donations. Many Mexican deaf families cannot afford food costs on top of travel.

👨‍👩‍👧 Participant Subsidies

Local deaf families will pay a small participation fee according to what each household can afford. Donations cover the gap so no family is turned away due to cost.

About New York Deaf Church

New York Deaf Church is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit religious organization based in Flushing, New York, dedicated to serving the deaf and hard of hearing community through faith-based programs and international mission work. All services are conducted in American Sign Language (ASL) and Korean Sign Language (KSL).

뉴욕농아인교회는 플러싱 뉴욕에 기반을 둔 비영리 종교단체로, 미국수화(ASL)와 한국수화(KSL)를 통해 농인 공동체에 영적 지도, 공동체 지원, 국제 선교 사역을 제공합니다.

Founded in New York and operating for over 40 years, our church has served deaf individuals and families across Korea, China, and the United States. In January 2026, we extended our mission to Mexico with the establishment of a deaf church in Cancún — now preparing for the historic first Deaf Family Faith Retreat in eastern Mexico.

New York Deaf Church Programs

The following are programs of New York Deaf Church (Flushing, NY) — not programs in Cancún.

Sunday Worship (New York)

Weekly services conducted entirely in American Sign Language at our Flushing, NY location. Open to all deaf and hearing individuals.

Community Support (New York)

Counseling, job assistance, and social services for deaf individuals and families in the New York metropolitan area.

Faith Education (New York)

Sign language–based Bible study and spiritual development programs for all ages at our New York church.

Mexico Deaf Mission

New York Deaf Church's international mission work in Cancún, Mexico — including the historic 2026 Deaf Family Faith Retreat.
