Our Mission

New York Deaf Church is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit religious organization dedicated to serving the deaf and hard of hearing community in the New York metropolitan area. We provide spiritual guidance, community support, and accessibility services in American Sign Language (ASL) and Korean Sign Language (KSL).

우리는 뉴욕 지역 농인 및 청각장애인 공동체를 섬기기 위해 헌신하는 종교단체입니다. 미국수화(ASL)와 한국수화(KSL)를 통해 영적 지도, 공동체 지원, 접근성 서비스를 제공합니다.

Our organization has been serving the deaf community for over 40 years, with operations spanning Korea, China, and the United States. We believe in the power of faith to transform lives and create inclusive communities where everyone is welcomed and valued.

Our Programs

Sunday Worship

Weekly worship services conducted entirely in American Sign Language with trained interpreters. All are welcome.

Community Support

Counseling, job placement assistance, and social services for deaf and hard of hearing individuals and families.

Educational Programs

Faith-based education and spiritual development programs for all ages in an accessible, inclusive environment.

International Mission

Supporting deaf communities in Mexico, Korea, and China through mission work and family faith retreats.
